Choose the correct present simple forms of ‘to be’ for the gaps below.




1A: [no answer] Wrong you a teacher? B: Yes, I [no answer] Wrong.

‣ We use you + are.
‣ We use I + am.

2A: [no answer] Wrong your name Marcus? B: Yes, it [no answer] Wrong.

Correct answer: Is/is
‣ Your name= it
‣ We use it + is.

‣ This = it.
‣ It = is/isn't.

‣ We use we + are.
‣ This = it.
‣ We use it + is.

Correct answer: Is/'s
‣ We use it + is.
‣ It is = it's.

7A: [no answer] Wrong your friends from the UK? B: No, [no answer] Wrong from the US.

Correct answer: Are/they are
‣ Your friends= they.
‣ We use they + are.

Correct answers: are/I'm
‣ We use you + are.
‣ We use I + am/'m.

1I [no answer] Wrong tea.

Correct answer: don't drink
‣ We make negative sentences with do not/don't (I/you/we/they) or does not/doesn't (he/she/it) + verb.

2She [no answer] Wrong two brothers.

Correct answer: has
‣ The verb have has the form has for he/she/it.

3Tom [no answer] Wrong work until 10 today.

Correct answer: doesn't start
‣ We make negative sentences with do not/don't (I/you/we/they) or does not/doesn't (he/she/it) + verb.
‣ When we use does or doesn’t, then the verb doesn’t add -s. We say 'Does he work?' (NOT Does he works?)

4[no answer] Wrong an instrument?

Correct answer: Do you play
‣ We make questions with do (I/you/we/they) or does (he/she/it) + subject + verb.

Correct answer: opens
‣ The museum= it. In present simple, we add -s or -es to the verb after he/she/it.

6Peter and Megan often [no answer] Wrong to the cinema.

Correct answer: go
‣ Peter and Megan= they. We don't add -s or -es to the verb after they.

7John [no answer] Wrong very late.

Correct answer: doesn't get up
‣ John= he. In present simple, we add -s or -es to the verb after he/she/it.
‣ When we use does or doesn’t, then the verb doesn’t add -s. We say 'Does he work?' (NOT Does he works?)

8What time [no answer] Wrong home in the morning?

‣ In present simple, we add -s or -es to the verb after he/she/it.
